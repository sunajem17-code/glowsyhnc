## PART 1: REDDIT INTELLIGENCE

### What r/Looksmaxxing / r/RateMe / r/selfimprovement users want

**#1 Complaint across all communities: Apps feel like scams**
- Umax's most common complaint: "submitted the same photo 3 times and got completely different scores every time"
- Paywall triggered AFTER you upload your photos — feels like a trap
- Advice is generic templates ("try a stronger jawline workout") — not personalized to your actual face
- Privacy fear: "now they have my email and photos and I can't delete them"

**Top feature requests (synthesized from reviews + forum discussions):**
1. Multiple free scans per week (not 1 scan/$3.99)
2. Consistent, calibrated scoring — same photo = same score
3. Per-feature breakdown (jawline score, canthal tilt score, symmetry score — not just "7.4")
4. Hairstyle try-on before the barber
5. Progress tracking with photo history ("show me I'm actually improving")
6. Nutrition and sleep inputs that actually affect recommendations
7. Body/physique analysis — not just face
8. Explain WHY — not just what your score is, but what it means and exactly what to do
9. Privacy transparency — "does my photo get deleted or kept?"
10. Celebrity lookalike / "see your 10/10 potential" visualization

**Pain points competitors haven't solved:**
- Mewing is the #1 searched topic (4 BILLION TikTok searches) — no dominant mewing tracker app exists
- Users want a daily habit system tied to their looks score (if sleep improves → does score improve?)
- Community rating ("honest strangers rating my photo") is requested constantly

Partner with barber chains or telehealth dermatology services. "Book a consultation based on your Ascendus skin score" — referral fee per booking. This already exists in fitness apps (Noom → doctors). First mover in looksmaxxing.

**5. Coaching Marketplace**
Let verified coaches offer 1:1 improvement plans inside the app. Take 20-30% cut. Users already want human expert validation. Bridges the gap between app advice and real expertise.

## COMPETITOR THREATS TO KNOW

**Threat #1 — RateByFresh scaling up**
OnPointFresh has their own app (RateByFresh) AND the editorial platform to rank it. They're building a content moat around their own product. If they invest in content SEO + TikTok, they'll be hard to dislodge.

**Threat #2 — Handsome AI (Mogged) going mainstream**
Currently PSL-community niche. But their tech (10+ category breakdown, consistent scoring, privacy-first, personalized plan) is genuinely the best in the market. If they simplify their branding and go mainstream, they're the most dangerous competitor.

**Threat #3 — Umax fixing their UX**
Umax has 3.5M+ downloads and brand recognition. If they fix the crashing, make scoring consistent, and improve the paywall UX, they'll retain their lead. Their head start is the threat, not their current product.

**Threat #4 — Apple / Google building this natively**
Low probability near-term, but Apple's health-adjacent features keep expanding. Worth watching.

**Threat #5 — Instagram / TikTok in-app filters**
Both platforms have AR filters that can "rate" or "glow-up" faces. They're free and native. The threat is if a filter goes viral and satisfies enough of the use case that users never download a dedicated app.
